# Miner's Fury credits

Who made the assets in this game. Keep this up to date when you add a pack.

## Art

- **Pixel Crawler packs** by **Anokolisa**: all the map tiles and props (Cave, Garden Environment, Library, Hideout, Castle Environment, Fairy Forest, Cemetery, Sewer, Forge, Desert, Free Pack 2.11). Terms: `assets/sprites/pc_tiles/Terms_Pixel_Crawler.txt` and `assets/sprites/pixel_crawler_desert/Terms.txt`.
- **Effects** by **Papoy Core** and **Fellor** (VFX Collector Pack, BitBlast Studio): spell and hit effects. Fellor's fire pack makes the Black Knight's fireball and lava, flame walls, the fire tornado and fire hits. (Fill in which other effects came from Papoy Core.)
- **Characters** by **Zerie** (Tiny RPG Character Asset Pack): the player swordsman, skeletons, orc, werewolf, werebear and slime.
  - The Storm, Venom and Flame swordsman looks and their swing effects are recolors of Zerie's swordsman.
  - Necromancer: check it's from the same pack, else add its source.
- **Ember Caldera monsters** by **Zerie** (Tiny RPG Character Asset Pack 02): Lava Slime, Hellbat, Ghostfire (and its beam) and the Black Knight boss (Black Knight_A).
  - The Lava Goo, Hellbat Wing and Charred Skull icons are cut from those monsters' frames.
- **Whips** (Leather, Storm, Venom, Flame) by **Zerie** (Tiny RPG Character Asset Pack 02): the whip and its lash marks are cut from Demoness_B's frames, put in the swordsman's hand and recolored.
